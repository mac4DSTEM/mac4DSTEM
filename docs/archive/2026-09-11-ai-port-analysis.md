## Area 6

DiffractionEmbedding.swift is a classical, CPU-only PCA + k-means baseline — no Metal, no Core ML, no learned model. Per pattern: log1p → per-pattern max-normalise → box-bin to binnedSize² (default 16 → d=256); stream the cube once with TilePrefetcher accumulating a double-precision sum and sum-of-outer-products; form the mean-centred d×d covariance; take the top-k eigenpairs; project every position (from a ≤512 MB cache, else a second streaming pass); then k-means++ seeded Lloyd's over the coordinates.
