# Spectroscopy room — UI/UX redesign spec (2026-10-06, main e41a003b, read-only review)

Scope: `mac4DSTEM/UI/Spectroscopy/*.swift` and the room's inspector sections only. The frozen shell (toolbar, sidebar,
inspector frame, infobar) is not specified here; what would need it is marked **owner picture needed**. Honesty labels stay:
the `unvalidated` word badge, "· no absorption", caveats, χ²ᵣ. Favour removal and quiet over addition.

Sources used. Apple HIG Materials / WWDC25 "Meet Liquid Glass" (wwdcnotes.com/…/wwdc25-219, createwithswift.com
"Exploring … Liquid Glass"): glass is "best used for the navigation layer that floats above the content"; avoid glass in
scrollable content and tables, avoid glass-on-glass, tint only the primary action; the Clear variant only "atop media-rich
content". HIG Color: colour sparingly, system colours, never colour alone for meaning. HIG Charts (summary): gridlines
sparingly, label marks directly where possible, descriptive text beside not inside the plot. HIG Inspectors (summaries):
narrower than content, grouped flat sections, show only relevant fields, advanced lower. HIG Typography macOS: 13 body,
10 the smallest text. The owner's own rules: `UI/InspectorRows.swift` header, `docs/archive/v4/window-design.md` §1/§8,
ADR 056, mock v2.1 notes. Velox (Thermo datasheet, owner's description, ADR 056): one processing window, maps own the
content, small tiles beside a dominant mix, element picks by periodic table, live cursor readout on the spectrum. AZtec /
ESPRIT (vendor pages): auto-ID is a proposal list the user confirms, never auto-filled tiles for everything above a bar.
Not to copy: Fiji/ImageJ-style floating tool windows and text-caption legends; Velox's Windows chrome (ribbons, grey panels).

## 1. Critique of r8-as.png (fresh open) and r8-bs.png (O Mg Al Si picked + Quantify)

Window 1100 × 690, dark. The structure now matches the accepted mock; what fails is density and voice.

1. **Seven dashed "proposed · Accept" tiles** (Cu Al O Eu Hf Co Ho) on open; five remain after Quantify. The mock the
   owner accepted shows two. Seven near-black noise boxes outweigh the four real maps; Eu/Hf/Ho on an Al alloy read as
   wrong to a microscopist before he has clicked anything. Rule: the map is the evidence (D2a) — but only for what a
   reader would look at; HIG Color / Buttons: seven blue "Accept" text links are seven prominent actions on one view.
2. **"Accept" twice**: on every tile and again in the inspector's Proposed row, as link-styled text. InspectorRows: buttons
   are bordered push buttons, never share a row, and the room's one prominent verb is Quantify in the toolbar.
3. **Spectrum chrome**: 9-pt axis text (below macOS's 10-pt floor), a vertical gridline at every keV and a horizontal at
   every decade, a boxed frame, and a 26-pt grey sentence under the plot ("grey: whole map · white: region · blue: model ·
   dotted: background"). HIG Charts: gridlines sparingly, do not explain the chart in a caption when the marks can carry it.
   On fresh open the plot spans 0–20 keV with nothing past 10 — the auto-zoom only follows *listed* lines.
4. **Residual strip**: clipped samples are filled red dots; on this spectrum they fuse into saturated red bars at every
   peak. Red is the app's error colour; here it marks a routine model miss. HIG Color: colour as meaning, sparingly.
5. **Quant panel after Quantify**: table (good) + bold ratio line + bold "χ²ᵣ 3318 (Pearson)" + a four-line caveat paragraph
   with two small buttons inside it + a truncated method disclosure. Four voices in 180 pt. The caveat and its buttons
   belong under one disclosure; χ²ᵣ is a result row, not a bold caption.
6. **Inspector Fit section**: a two-line note "not applied: no thickness is typed (nm): type one in the Quantify inspector"
   — a paragraph, and "Quantify inspector" no longer exists (false wording, allowed to fix anywhere). Beam energy row holds
   three things (caption "from the file", field, unit). Auto ID row holds a word badge and a switch; colour as a word.
7. **ColorMix caption** "O · Al · Si (Mg not ticked)" is a sentence on a map header; the unticked box on the Mg tile already
   says it.
8. **Periodic table**: 10-pt symbols in 21-pt cells, the fold "La–Lu · Ac–Lr" as 10-pt grey text with its own tiny chevron
   — a second disclosure vocabulary beside the sections'. ADR 056 asked for about 22-pt cells.
9. **Map header controls**: two `.small` segmented pickers (int/net, rect/polygon) with no visual grouping; the glyph
   pair is 9 pt. Acceptable in a 26-pt header, but they should read as one tool group.
10. **Overlays on maps** ("Region · 572 px · live", "Pick elements to map", scale bar) are black 55 % rectangles. HIG
    Materials allows a thin material exactly here — atop media — and nowhere else in this room.
11. **Hover text "Hf+Hf sum? (or Zr Kα)"** sits inside the plot, grey italic, top right, with no marker visible for it at
    this zoom; a question floating in the data area. Suspects should be markers with labels only when their energy is in view.
12. Un-Mac: `.link` buttons in content, text in `.white` with shadow on tiles (fine on media), 8-pt "±3" label, the "res σ"
    axis label; none of these use system text styles. Nothing here needs custom chrome to look modern: system materials
    on overlays, SF Symbols for states, monospaced digits, one accent hue, and generous dark tiles already give the look.

## 3. Decisions taken as the owner's delegate (he overrules on sight)

- D-1 Cap proposed tiles at three (above). The periodic table remains the complete proposal list; the grid is for looking.
- D-2 Accept lives on the tile's checkbox and in one inspector button; the `.link` style leaves the room entirely.
- D-3 No red in this room. Error states use `xmark.circle` secondary with help; clipping is a tick, not a dot.
- D-4 Legend-by-caption is gone; direct labels and the Show menu carry it. If a reader needs more, the plot's help has it.
- D-5 Materials only on overlays over maps (thin), never on the inspector, table, headers or spectrum.
- D-6 Typography floor 10 pt everywhere in the room; inspector text 13 pt unchanged; pane headers `.callout` semibold unchanged.
- D-7 Order of inspector sections stays Elements · Region · Fit · Map display · Export · Expert (ADR 056 §3).
