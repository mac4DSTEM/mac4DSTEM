(330 × 330 × 256 × 256 float32, 28 556 340 121 bytes, on the owner's exFAT SSD), binned × 4 by sum, against the


## What was predicted, and what happened

Part 6 predicted 108 900 patterns with max relative Δ ≤ 1e-6 and a far larger transposed Δ. **Refuted on the first 121
patterns**: 0 bit-identical, max |Δ| 193 565, max relative 3.86.

1. **Diagnosis tried first:** the app's DM4 reader orders this file's axes differently from py4DSTEM. **Refuted**: ncempy's own
   memmap of the raw (the layout py4DSTEM's `read_dm` uses), sum-binned in float32, differs from the file by the same amounts
   at the same places (193 565.5 at scan (30, 120); 80 500.1 at (0, 0)). The app's read and ncempy's agree.
2. **What the file is:** with a float32 sum, 4 081–4 084 of 4 096 binned pixels per pattern equal the file bit for bit; the rest
   sit at the same 15 detector positions in every pattern. py4DSTEM's `filter_hot_pixels` replaces a fixed mask by each
   pattern's 3 × 3 local median. Predicted: the file's value at those 15 positions is that median of the sum-binned raw —
   **held, 90 of 90** over six patterns. The refuter then ran it over the whole scan: sum-bin, replace the 15 in py4DSTEM's
   order — the file bit for bit, 0 mismatches; and py4DSTEM's own mask step on the mean binned pattern at `thresh = 8`
   returns exactly these 15 (any threshold between 7.60 and 9.80 does). The notebook on the SSD
   (`LMN_MA_4DSTEM/code/4DSTEM_first_steps_loading_preprosessing.ipynb`) has a `filter_hot_pixels(thresh = 8)` cell after
   `bin_Q`, but as saved it is for another dataset at bin 2 and that cell shows no execution: it shows where the step
   comes from, not that it ran for this file. The data shows that.
3. **The full run** (probe extended to keep, per detector position, the largest relative difference and how often the file
   holds the median; predicted: the same 15 lead, a gap of three decades below them, every differing pattern a median,
   footprint under 500 MB) — **held**:

## What it means

- **The app's raw DM4 read and its binning are right**: at the 4 081 unfiltered positions numpy's float32 sum of the raw is
  the file bit for bit over the whole scan (444 420 900 values), and the app's row-major accumulation accounts for its own
  residual exactly: 54 % of values differ in the last bits, the worst by 1.1e-4 absolute on a 4 × 4 block spanning −145 to
  +246 that sums to 0.33 (the raw has negative pixels, so a sum can nearly cancel).
- **The file named "unfiltered" is hot-pixel filtered.** 15 detector pixels are replaced by a local median in every pattern.
  One of them is (32, 32), the direct beam's peak (mean 7.6 × 10⁵ counts, flagged for exceeding its neighbourhood's second
  brightest by 9 323 against a threshold of 8); at least four pairs of the others sum to about (64, 64) — the mask is the
  local maxima of the mean pattern, not isolated defects. Any cube passed through that filter would carry its own such
  mask. Whether that matters for a result is the owner's call; it is a property of his files, not of the app.
- **The read rule**: a 28 GB DM4 on a local volume opens and streams at a footprint of tens of MB on this Mac. "Never open
  > 2 GB through an unproven path" stays a rule for unproven paths; this path is now proven on the physical SSD
  (Gate B's open question of 2026-09-24). A vanished volume is still a SIGBUS with no dialog.
