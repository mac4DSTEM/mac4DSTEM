**S14-D: Gate D on the origin refine step with non-flat (ringed) probes. Diagnosis + pre-registration (2026-09-30; nothing run yet; YELLOW: measure, refute, propose, never commit a moved number)**

**1. Defect statement (a candidate, not established).** `measureOrigin`'s refine (`Shaders/OriginMeasure.metal:103-133`) is an iterated centre of mass in a window `win = max(1.2 r, r + 1.5)`, 4 passes, from a block-sum seed. On a bullseye probe (disk, dark gap, ring) `r` (probeSize, an equivalent-area radius) reaches into the ring, so `win` cuts through the ring. The centre map then has gain > 1 at the true centre (shift the window toward +x: ring mass enters at +x, leaves at -x, the centroid moves further +x): the true centre is an UNSTABLE fixed point and the iteration walks away along the ring; the seed only decides where it ends. Recorded, not yet mine: S14 shipped plane mean on bullseye_sim (125.82, 125.54) vs the seed-free mean-DP centre (127.01, 127.50); refuter, 9 bullseye_sim patterns, one Gaussian seed: 1 pass 0.6-1.0 px, 4 passes 2.8-3.7 px, 10 passes further from a 3 r CoM (the refuter's `ref-s14/verdict.md` is not on disk; those numbers are quoted from the brief). Rivals to exclude: (R1) slow convergence, not divergence (gain < 1, 4 passes too few); (R2) the block seed is wrong and the refine forgets nothing (S14: seed changes moved the fit 1.2-1.6 px on bullseye); (R3) the reference is wrong, not the app; (R4) plane-fit trimming.
