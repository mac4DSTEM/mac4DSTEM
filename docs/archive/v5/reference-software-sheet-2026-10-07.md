against … this needs to be done properly." Survey with every claim sourced: `reference-software-survey-2026-10-07.md` (a Sonnet research agent, 2026-10-07;
unverified items marked there). The principle stays py4DSTEM's: one pinned open reference per product, a harness under `tools/`, parity numbers dated.
