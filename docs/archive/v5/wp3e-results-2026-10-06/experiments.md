## What the evidence supports (candidate fixes; none implemented)
1. 1a presentation (sum label first) is needed regardless: on the synthetic the Ar question is a plain Al+Al pile-up (Al listed) at 13-15 x L_D.
2. 1b parents rule: the mechanism is proven on 1339 (Zr = Hf+Hf, Hf parent while Hf is Al+Fe, Fe is Mn+O, Mn is Al+Ba, Ba a proposal at 1.03 x L_D). The registered fix (b) removes the Zr question by removing its parent but (inference, unobserved) leaves a plain Zr proposal (5727 vs L_D 375); so (b) alone moves the phantom from a question to a proposal and needs its own run, ideally with F3.1/F3.3.
3. F3.1 (reuse neighbour()): supported, cheap, and the only registered item that removes Tm/V/Lu/Hf-at-Cu (9 of 98), but it clears only that family.
4. F3.2 max-scaling: partial: 37 -> 22 non-Cu proposals on the nine owner pools, Mg unaffected, Cu unaffected, but Eu/Hf survive on every whole/matrix pool, the AlCoCu control is unchanged, and the greedy set stays unstable on 1456 and several network pools. Not a standalone cure; sqrt chi2r alone is harmful. The unmeasured next registration is a local (under-the-column) misfit statistic for the survivors (Eu, Hf), as the pre-reg's M3b says; nothing here tests it.
