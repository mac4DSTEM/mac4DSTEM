# The v4.1 plan — lanes, order, what runs in parallel (registered 2026-09-30 night, after ADR 050)

The plan AND the log: a lane's session appends one line under it when it lands (commit, gate numbers, what deviated).
Every lane is pre-registered here at the level a `/pickup` needs; the session writes the finer prediction before each run.
Rules of the road are unchanged (CLAUDE.md): Gate D where a number can move, an independent refuter, break every new test,
no new surface while the "Unverified on screen" row is non-empty except by a session's own drive, `main` only, the owner
pushes. Sonnet 5.5 implementers on disjoint write-sets under one Fable 5.1 supervisor per lane; a Fable refuter per Gate B.

## The lanes

**P — Polish room (1 session).** The drives' list (`open-items.md` § S4/S5): the wording items, "px" wrap, lineage order,
legend counts, crop-warning axis order, the drag sentence; A1's park-the-file's-centre; A6's empty-circle predicate; A3's
name-shadowing order (imported CIF wins); A5's red line names the variable; **the bin-2 aperture ring off-centre (Gate D
first, a real defect)**; ratify the two landed controls by looking. Driven on a scratch build, shot by shot; empties the
"Unverified on screen" row (Fit anyway after reopen; pinch zoom; restored-origin branch; the review sheet width).
Write-set: `UI/` rooms (not the five frozen files), `App/AppState+Calibration`, `Session/`. Gate: unit + drive.

**Q — Science quick wins (1 session, +1 if the fix is funded).** (a) The Q badge reads "unchecked" when the shell ratio
disagrees or only one shell exists; the app path checked on the demo cube **and the owner's raw Al–Mg–Si cube** (does his
[001] Al read ≈ 1.41?). (b) The S20 true-Q rerun (minutes): decides candidate F's regression and whether the demo cube is
truth-bearing for ACOM; F itself is After v4.1 (4.5× slower). (c) C14: the origin window-sensitivity quantity in the log +
a test, no verdict change. (d) If (a) shows his cube mis-assigned: the ring-assignment Gate D (match the measured ratio
sequence), refuter, harness re-pin. Write-set: `Core/Crystal` Q calibration, `App/AppState+ACOM`, `UI/PrepareSettings`
badge. Gate: Gate D + refuter for (d); unit for (a)–(c).

**T — Training becomes useful (2–3 sessions; the owner's priority).**
T1 *The kernel question first* (the owner: "py4DSTEM does it, no? figure out why and how"): py4DSTEM correlates with the
**measured probe image itself** (vacuum probe → kernel), not with a synthetic disk sized by a radius estimate. The app
has that source (File probe / vacuum scan / measured ROI) but defaults to Synthetic, and its measured radius reads a
ring's inner shoulder (S21). Measure on the bullseye cube and the ten pinned datasets: default = the measured probe
image when one exists, synthetic only without one; against S21's outer-edge patch. No fancy rule; normal probes must not
move (the pinned harnesses say so). Gate D + Gate B; the bullseye harness re-pinned with the stated reason.
T2 *Effectiveness, pre-registered*: the metric is held-out recall/precision at 2 px on the owner's 66-position set plus the
committed 40 (two labellers — the inter-labeller check comes free); the recipe's knobs (steps, learning rate,
augmentation, label count) measured one at a time on the M5; ship what wins under D7, nothing else. T3 *The room*: labels
import (JSON) beside export, the split visible, a kernel hint when the probe is ring-structured, the review sheet seen.
Depends on: the owner's File-probe run (the diagnosis' own test). Write-set: `Core/Analysis/ProbeKernel`, `DiskDetection`
defaults, `Training/`, `UI/MapSettings` (Bragg Disks room). Gate: Gate D + Gate B, scientific, drive.

**R — Reconstruction parity (3–4 sessions; independent of every other lane).**
R1 the ptychography probe takes a defocus (and the fitted aberrations), seeded from the parallax fit or typed; compared to
py4DSTEM on the graphene cube at defocus 600 Å (record: `parallax-ptycho-graphene-2026-09-30/`). R2 the difference map:
Gate D on why it diverges on that cube (py4DSTEM's converges) — fix or, if the diagnosis says the algorithm as ported
cannot, say so and remove it with the reason. R3 ground truth: the synthetic fixtures of `tools/singleslice-ptychography-
test` have a known object — extend them with a defocused probe so both methods are scored against truth, not only against
py4DSTEM; the graphene cube stays the real-data check. R4 parallax's two residuals: the edge vignette (full-frame 0.36 vs
interior 1.000) and the (2,1) higher-order pair (≈ 0 vs ≈ 600 Å) — Gate D each, fix or label. R5 both driven in the
Reconstruction room on the graphene cube. Write-set: `Core/Analysis/{Ptychography*,Parallax*}`, `UI/ReconstructionSettings`,
`App/AppState+PhaseContrast`, the two harnesses. Gate: Gate D + Gate B + scientific + drive.

**X — Preprocessing of raw data, in the app (2 sessions).** X1 *drive what exists*: open the raw 28 GB DM4, measure the
origin, Preprocess & Export at bin 4, and compare the result with py4DSTEM's file with `tools/dm4-parity-probe --parity`
(bit-identical except the 15 filtered pixels is the prediction). X2 add what py4DSTEM's preprocessing has and the sheet
lacks: scan stride, an optional hot-pixel filter **named as such** (py4DSTEM's algorithm, its mask reported), a detector
crop; the output opens directly in the app or binned/cropped on load. X3 the entry the owner asked for: "Preprocess Raw
Data…" as its own sheet beside Open with Options — **mock first** (a picture he accepts; it is a rework of the existing
sheet, not a new room). Write-set: `UI/ExportSheet`, `Support/ResultExport` export path, `Core/Data` writer, the parity
probe. Gate: the parity comparison as a harness (gated), unit, drive.

**K — Keep the cube in memory (1–2 sessions; the one new control).** K1 a mock of the per-open toggle in the load
configurator showing "28.6 of 64 GB" with a warning above ≈ 0.5 of RAM (the residency record's slope) — the owner
accepts the picture. K2 build: the request reaches `.resident` (the plumbing exists, `AppState+Open` "nothing in the UI
requests .resident yet"), the status strip says resident, Release cube reachable. K3 drive on the 28 GB cube (a virtual
image 84 ms vs 6 s). K4 *after v4.1*: what a resident cube allows (live aperture, live detection) — a design note, no code.
Write-set: `UI/LoadConfigurator`, `App/AppState+Open` residency, `Session/`. Gate: unit + drive.

**F — The review's findings (1–2 sessions).** After the owner's sitting on the cloud review's table: the picked findings
by module, Sonnet implementers on disjoint files, one Fable supervisor; a finding that can move a number is a Gate D.

**Z — Release v4.1.0 (1 session, after a second short review pass on the diff since the first — the four finished pieces).** `run-tests.sh all` on the release commit; the "Unverified on screen" row empty; the
owner's own analysis end to end in a release-candidate build; CHANGELOG; the fresh notary password; DMG per
`docs/releasing.md`.

## Order, and what runs beside what

Three lanes at a time at most (one supervisor each; commits serialised through the unit gate; write-sets disjoint):

| Slot | In parallel | Owner meanwhile |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (next) | **P** polish room · **Q** science quick wins · **R1** ptychography probe | fires the cloud review; the File-probe training run; the "do my numbers read those pixels?" check; re-exports Si-SiGe |
| 2 | **T1** kernel question · **R2 + R3** difference map + ground truth · **X1 + X2** preprocessing | reads the review's findings table |
| 3 | **T2** training effectiveness · **R4 + R5** parallax residuals, drive · **X3 mock + K1 mock** | accepts or redraws the two mocks; the findings sitting |
| 4 | **F** findings · **K2 + K3** toggle built and driven · **T3** the room | his own analysis in the RC build |
| 5 | **Z** release | the push, the notary |

Dependencies that order this: T waits for the File-probe run (the diagnosis' test); X3 and K2 wait for a mock the owner
accepted; F waits for the review; Z waits for all. R is independent from slot 1 to slot 3. About five slots; one slot is
one evening or night session of three supervised lanes.
