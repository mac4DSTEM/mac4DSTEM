**S14: origin coarse seed vs a Gaussian-argmax comparator. Pre-registration (2026-09-30, nothing run; YELLOW: measure, refute, propose, never commit a moved number)**

**1. Question.** `measureOrigin` (`Shaders/OriginMeasure.metal`) seeds its iterated CoM with the argmax of non-overlapping block sums, bin = round(r). py4DSTEM seeds with `argmax(gaussian_filter(dp, sigma = r))` (`origin.py:212`, `mode="nearest"` at `:284`). Recorded: 28/169, 29/195, 2/169 positions of three cubes miss by > 1 px vs that seed (`q-calibration-design.md` §9; the 12-stride samples of `origin-kernel-twin.py`, cubes `downsample_Si_SiGe_exp`, `Particle_1…bin8`, `COPL…`). Does a filtered-argmax seed (box, O(qy·qx); design §4 "~2–3×") move the FINAL fitted origin (`fitOriginTrimmed`, plane, what `tiledRun` returns), on which datasets, by how much, at what cost?

**2. Datasets: every cube `measureOrigin` reaches that exists on this Mac** (all via `tiledRun`; shape ry×rx×qy×qx)
- Noisy, the recorded misses: `downsample_Si_SiGe_exp` 50×200×128², `Particle_1` 90×45×128².
- Clean: `polycrystal_2D_WS2` 128²×128², `sim_Au_data_all_binned` polyAu + AuNanoplatelet 100×84×125², `calibrationData_bullseyeProbe` polyAu + simulation 100×84×250², `Si-SiGe_calibrated` 77×17×448×480, `Au_ref_ROI15` 205×310×64², `Al_Mg_Si_060…bin_4` 330²×64² (the `-ellipse-20260925` copy holds the same 4D array: excluded).
- Application data: `thronsen-datasetA/datasetA_stride3.h5` 171²×128², `demo-dataset/AlMgSi_demo.h5` 100²×128², and `--demo-fixture`'s synthetic (`DemoFourDDataSource`, 12²×64², sub-pixel descan drift).
- NOT on this Mac: `COPL…` (the 2/169), `SPED_MgO`, `twisted_bilayer_graphene`. COPL's recorded miss cannot be re-measured; that row stays a quotation, not a result.

**3. Variants** (one probe, one read of each tile; kernels copied from the shipped shader under `$SP/s14/`, source tree untouched; refine step byte-identical to shipped)
- V0 shipped block-sum seed. V1 box-argmax seed, side 2·round(r)+1 (disk-matched), window clipped at the detector edge (a DEVIATION from `nearest`, reported by miss location). V2 box, variance-matched side ≈ √12·r (secondary; V1 is the registered primary).
- G: py4DSTEM's Gaussian-argmax seed (scipy `gaussian_filter(dp, r, mode="nearest")`, truncate 4σ, ≥ 50 patterns per cube cross-checked against a Swift copy) fed to the SAME refine step. G is the comparator, not the truth. Truth proxy where needed: iterated 3 r CoM from G (WIDE).
- Comparator validity first: V0 through the GPU must equal the numpy twin (`origin-kernel-twin.py` `NEW`) to ≤ 1e-3 px on the 12-stride sample and reproduce the recorded 28/169 and 29/195 within ± 3 (else the comparator or the recorded number is wrong and the run stops there). r comes from `probeSize` on the mean DP, as `tiledRun` does.

**4. Statistics, per dataset, full scan.** Seed misses (> 1 px vs G) for V0, V1, V2, raw seed and after refine; per-position |Δ measured| V1−V0 (median, p95, max); final fitted origin |Δ| V1−V0 over all positions (median, max) and of the scan mean; `excludedFraction` and `fullScanResidual` each variant; whether `originFitIsSane`'s verdict flips. Cost: median of 15 dispatches at the app's real tile grid (the `coarse-cost` method), coarse-only (kernels cut before refine) and whole kernel, V0/V1/V2.

**5. Predictions (written before any run)**
- (a) Seed misses vs G, full scan, V0 → V1: Si_SiGe_exp 17 % → ≤ 5 %; Particle_1 15 % → ≤ 5 %; the reproduced 28/169 and 29/195 fall to ≤ 8 and ≤ 8. Residual V1 misses are near-ties between blobs of similar filtered mass. The box and Gaussian are the same low-pass at the disk scale, so V1 ≈ G is the expected mechanism; V2 no better than V1 by more than 2 points.
- (b) Clean cubes (WS2, both Au, bullseye, Al-Mg-Si 060, Au_ref, demo cube, fixture): 0 seed misses for V0 and V1; per-position |Δ measured| median < 0.02 px, max < 0.10 px (seed differs by < bin/2 and the iterated window forgets it; Gate B: new − wide ≤ 0.07 px); final fitted origin median |Δ| < 0.02 px, max < 0.10 px.
- (c) Noisy cubes: the plane fit's trimming hides most seed changes. Si_SiGe_exp (broad failure, 84 % beyond r): fitted origin max |Δ| < 1 px, `fullScanResidual` stays within ± 10 % of 11.655, BLOCK unchanged. Particle_1: fitted origin max |Δ| < 2 px, `fullScanResidual` within ± 25 % of 18.475, `excludedFraction` moves by ≤ 5 points from its shipped value, BLOCK unchanged. Thronsen and Al-Mg-Si 060: not a seed problem; max |Δ| < 0.10 px.
- (d) Cost: V1 coarse-only 2–4× the shipped coarse step (fewer than 3× is a coin flip), whole kernel ≤ 1.6×; gets worse with r (thread-private column sums up to qx floats: 480 on Si-SiGe_calibrated may spill).

**6. Bar to propose (all required).** Seed misses on the two noisy cubes fall by ≥ 50 %; no dataset worse (no fitted-origin |Δ| above the bounds in (b)/(c), no `originFitIsSane` flip, no clean-cube seed miss introduced); the per-dataset final-origin change table (median, max |Δ| px) is complete; V1 cost ≤ 3× the shipped coarse step at every dataset's tile grid. If met: a READY PATCH (not applied) and the list of every harness it would repin. Candidates read from the code, each to be run against the patched kernel before the list is written: `virtual-detector-test` (`origin_measurement_truth`, 0.02 px), `two-spec-analysis-test` (P4, 0.001 px), `load-spec-calibration`, `virtual-detector-residency`, `resident-cropped-view`, `virtual-detector-memory-test`, `ProbeSizeTests`, `training-dataset-campaign`, `real-acom-benchmark`.

**7. What refutes.** Miss reduction < 50 % on either noisy cube (the seed is not the mechanism or the box is not the Gaussian at this r). Any clean cube with a V1 seed miss or a fitted-origin |Δ| > 0.10 px. Any `originFitIsSane` verdict flip. Cost > 3× on any dataset (then the recommendation is "not the box, or a scratch-buffer design", not a patch). If V1 matches G but G itself is off the beam on the noisy cubes (WIDE from G ≠ WIDE from V0 and neither near the fit), record that the comparator is not a truth and propose nothing; a null result on origin change is not a mechanism (Si_SiGe_exp's failure may be descan, not the seed).

**8. Limits stated now.** A production box seed needs per-thread or scratch storage the current kernel lacks; the probe's thread-private array is a measurement device, not a design. Thresholds above (bins, ± %) are these datasets' and this Mac's (8 GB, tile heights from RAM), not the method's. Heavy work under the heavy lock, footprint guard 2.5 GB, one build, nothing in the working tree.
