# 052 — v4.5 ships as it stands; v5.0 is the EDX suite

Dates: 2026-10-05

Status: **accepted** (owner, 2026-10-05). Supersedes ADR 051 item 1 (no release before the owner's drive); amends ADR 049
(the freeze ends for EDX once v4.5 is out).

## Decision

1. **v4.5 is released as it stands — no drive first.** The unpublished 4.1.0 / build 8 cut of 2026-10-02 is renamed
   4.5.0 / 8 (build 8 was never notarized or distributed). The release is the steps in `docs/releasing.md`; the owner's
   notary password, push, tag and GitHub release stay his.
2. **v5.0 opens with an EDX suite** — the theme ADR 051 named for "v5 or v6". After v4.5 the freeze of ADR 049 is lifted
   for this one theme; every other frozen theme stays frozen.
3. **Fixed by the owner** (not re-opened by a session):
   - the term **"4D-STEM + EDX"** — one scan that records, at each probe position, both a diffraction pattern (kx, ky) and
     an EDX spectrum (energy); plain EDX spectrum images (x, y, E) are in scope too. Never "5D".
   - a **seventh workspace room named "Spectroscopy"** — a change to the frozen shell (ADR 035, ADR 046's six rooms), so it
     is built only against a mock the owner accepts.
   - the basis: **HyperSpy / eXSpy / RosettaSciIO** (all GPL-3.0) ported to Swift and Metal as py4DSTEM was, pinned to one
     upstream commit, with `DEVIATION` notes; other open tools may inform.
   - the inputs that matter first: his lab's **GMS (STEMx) 4D-STEM + EDX acquisitions (expected `.dm4`)** and **standalone
     Velox `.emd`** EDX files.
4. **No implementation is committed yet.** The owner asked for the full picture first: the market, the open tools, the
   literature, how Velox and GMS do it, the input paths and the method options are in `docs/archive/v5/edx-dossier-2026-10-05.md`, and the
   choices are put to him as decision cards on the Board. A v5.0 session starts only from his answers.

## Consequences

- The ROADMAP moves EDX from "frozen" to v5.0; its pre-registration (ROADMAP § How a feature is done) follows the owner's
  answers to the v5.0 cards.
- The polish candidates of ADR 051 (the deferred room-switch class, small wording) are release-noted in v4.5 and carried
  in `open-items.md`.
- The v4.1 plan record (Slots 1–4⅞) is closed; the Board's v4.1 track is archived.
