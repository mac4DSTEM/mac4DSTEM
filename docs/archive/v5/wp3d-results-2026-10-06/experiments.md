Method: the repo's own EDSFit model (LS, refined axis, 0.2 keV to min(axis end, beam, 20 keV), escape peaks on): every element in the list gets its Ka/La/Ma groups in range (EDSLineModel.build); net(B)/net(A) is the ratio of two group areas of one element in one fit, sigma from the fit covariance. B1 needs a sibling in range and above the noise; sibling families that fall outside the 0.2-20 keV window are not in the ratio.
Not run: B2 (a geometry rule, no number to measure), the S2c synthetic (needs the python generator; time), the SEM TM002 set (not named in the pre-reg).

## Summary of what stands
Item 1: H1b (weighting / local misfit absorbed by the stiff continuum) accounts for about 55-65 % of the matrix deficit (Run 1, refuter not met) but not P1.1's bar; the Al Ka shoulder column, ML and the 1.55-1.66 mask are the same lever (P2.4 held). H1a (Si step) is supported by one pool only (1339 matrix 12.6 +- 3.7 %, 3.4 sigma); its pure-Al arm could not run. After ML + the Al column (V10) the data/model step from 1.69-1.815 to 1.85-1.975 keV is 0.88 (1339 matrix), 0.77 / 0.78 (1840 / 1909 matrix), 0.80 / 0.79 / 0.80 (whole), 0.93-0.98 (network): it differs between files by about 0.1 (registered H1a refuter: "size differs between files by more than its sigma" -- NOT the registered measurement, since 1909/1840 matrix are not Si-free; stated as an indication only). In 1909/1840 the continuum is too LOW at 2.17-2.40 keV (data/model 1.07-1.20) while too high at 1.85-1.975, so the 1.85-2.4 keV misfit there is a trough, not a step to a plateau. H1d, H1e were not tested (refuted by magnitude in the pre-reg). H1c (specimen Si edge) not tested.
Item 2: H2d refuted (width). H2c refuted (split position/absence changes the excess by < 20 %). H2b not supported (no feature on Si Ka network or Cu Ka at +100 eV). H2a/H2e not decided: the feature is real data (present, 0.54-0.59 % window / 1.1-1.4 % column, in 9/9 pools), sits at +80...+90 eV (10-15 eV below the registered +95...+110 and close to the Al Kb position, +71-73 eV: a free column at the table or true Kb position fits within 0.3-2.1 % RSS of the best), is not visible on the one other Al spectrum available, and has no pure-Al reference. Lu is not supported (H2e / V7 unchanged).
Item 3: B1 (L/K family consistency) is not a usable bar (P3.3 failed: the ratio spans > 10x, even for one element); M/L rests on Pt alone. B2 + B3 would be what lands, per the pre-reg's own rule.
Candidate fix the evidence supports (NOT implemented): (i) item 2/1 together: a measured Al K high-side column tied to Al Ka (ReferenceShape .lineAmplitude, fraction ~1.1-1.4 % at +80...+90 eV, i.e. a Kb/Ka of about 0.024-0.027 total against the table's 0.0132 if it is Kb), shipped "unvalidated" with its source named; it removes the excess and moves the 1.85-1.975 keV data/model by +0.19...+0.26 under LS. Because the column and ML both only partly close the deficit, and the fitted shoulder is not seen on the second Al set, the fix needs the owner's pure-Al spectrum before it is a default. (ii) weighting: Poisson-ML moves the same quantity by +0.15 and the 1.55-1.66 mask by +0.18...+0.24, so the estimator choice is a registered separate decision; (iii) a detector Si step column only after the pure-Al measurement (one pool says 8-18 %); (iv) item 3 lands as B2 + B3 (label as "unexplained excess beside Al Ka", never an element; do not withhold at% on it), the sheet decides.
