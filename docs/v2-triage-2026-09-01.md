**Prepared 2026-09-01** from
[`docs/archive/2026-08-31-review/findings.json`](archive/2026-08-31-review/findings.json)
(baseline `24c13d3`). This is a **recommendation for an owner decision**, not a
scope change. Nothing here is scheduled until the release owner says which group
is v2 and which is v2.x.

**Update, 2026-09-01:** `app-appstate-01` has since been **reproduced at runtime**
(see its row), and `core-analysis-physics-01` was reproduced, fixed and cleared
through Gate D + Gate B (see its row).

**Read the counts honestly.** 39 confirmed of 75 entries; duplicates are retained
for traceability, so this is not 39 distinct defects. "Confirmed" means supported
by source inspection unless an entry records an experiment. Two now have
experiments: `app-appstate-01` remains open; `core-analysis-physics-01` is
resolved. Several of the rest will shrink or vanish under a controlled
reproduction. That is why every group below names its gate.

The 34 *narrowed* findings are deliberately out of this list. They are not
dismissed; they are the second pass.

---

## The decision in one paragraph

**Groups A and B are the v2 case.** They are the findings that make the app say
something quantitatively untrue on screen or in an exported file, or mishandle a
file — which is exactly what §1's fourth commitment promises they cannot:
*"nothing displayed or exported can be misread as a quantitative claim the app
isn't making."* Everything else can ship in v2.x without making that sentence
false. **Group D is small, cheap, and should ride along with A/B** — those are
green-but-worthless tests, the failure mode this repo has been burned by three
times, and they are how you would catch a regression in the A/B fixes.

## Group C — py4DSTEM parity deviations → **recommend v2.x**

Real, but they change agreement with the reference implementation rather than
making the app assert something false. Most want a `DEVIATION` note and a
decision, not a fix.

`core-compute-shaders-01` (matrix DFT wraps one Fourier frequency on each odd
axis, med) · `core-compute-shaders-02` (CoM clamps negative intensities,
undocumented, med) · `core-analysis-physics-06` (parallax basis uses BF-centroid
vs array-center, med) · `core-analysis-physics-07` (strain residual weighted RMS
vs weighted mean distance, low) · `core-compute-shaders-03` (origin refinement
includes exact-radius pixels, low) · `core-analysis-detect-03` (`probeSize` uses
`sorted[n/2]` where py4DSTEM uses `np.median`, low)

**One caveat against deferring all of them:** `core-compute-shaders-01` is in
subpixel disk positioning, which strain is fitted from. If it biases positions
rather than just perturbing them, it belongs in Group A. **Measure it before
deciding** — that is a cheap Gate D and it is the only Group C item worth
resolving before the cut.

## What this does to the date

Groups A + B + D is **roughly 5–7 sessions** at this repo's gate discipline —
most of Group A needs Gate D first (a controlled reproduction before any fix),
and the two `Core/Crystal` items need a fixture. That is the honest cost of the
release claim, and it is separate from S22, TB1 sittings 2–4, TB2 and S20.

Cutting Groups C, E and F to v2.x removes none of it — those were never the
blocking set. **The lever, if a date is needed, is whether every Group A item is
v2 or whether some ship as documented, disclosed limitations.** That is an owner
call, and it is the one worth making deliberately.
