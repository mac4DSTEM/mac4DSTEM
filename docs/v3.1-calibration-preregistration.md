## The theme, sequenced

`ROADMAP.md` priority-1 "Calibration foundation" is four sub-items. Gate B
capacity is one campaign at a time, so they are sequenced, least-risk first.
The **origin validity mask leads** (owner, 2026-09-17): it is owed by a
recorded decision, it clears a live open item, and — done as pure disclosure —
it moves **no shipped scientific number**, so it lands the v3.1 scaffolding
without spending the single Gate B slot.

1. **Origin validity mask** — this document. Disclosure only, unit gate.
2. Recover the discarded CoM beam centre in `probeSize` — small, Gate D (moves
   the origin). `probeSize` returns `(r, x0, y0)`; both callers keep `r` and
   drop `x0/y0` (`OriginCalibration.swift:516,573`). **Diagnostic entry point
   (read-only, 2026-09-17):** py4DSTEM feeds `get_probe_size`'s `(x0,y0)` in as
   the *default origin that centres the probe cross-correlation kernel*
   (`braggvectors/probe.py:502-556`, `if origin is None`). The port already
   centres the kernel **per scan position** on the fitted origin map
   (`DiskDetection.swift:587-588`), falling back to `referenceOrigin` only when
   no map exists — so the discarded CoM centre's likely role is the **pre-fit
   `referenceOrigin` seed** (currently the detector geometric centre), not a
   replacement for the per-position fitted origins. The Gate D owes: confirm what
   `referenceOrigin` is today, then measure whether seeding it with the CoM centre
   changes detected peaks — **needs the owner's real data** (demo cube + the
   gitignored training cubes), so the experiment cannot run unattended.
3. Beamstop-tolerant origin (`get_origin_friedel`) — new py4DSTEM port, Gate D
   + a parity harness; unblocks Medipix/EMPAD beamstop data.
4. Vacuum probe from a separate scan — largest; new data-loading path + UI; the
   real fix for the MgO disk-radius finding. Its own pre-registration when reached.

Each later item is pre-registered as its own step before its code; this file
registers item 1 in full.

## Item 1 — Origin validity mask

**What it is.** `OriginMaps` today carries the *scalar* `excludedFraction`
(`Calibration.swift:456`) and nothing about **which** scan positions the robust
trim excluded. But the trim already computes that per-position mask:
`TrimmedFit.kept: [Bool]` (`OriginCalibration.swift:188`), and `excludedFraction`
is *derived from it* (`:204-206`). The mask is discarded — the two `OriginMaps`
constructors pass `fitted.excludedFraction` and `fitted.keptResidual` and drop
`fitted.kept` (`:540-544`, `:594-598`). This increment surfaces that already-
computed mask on the product. Nothing more: the fitted origins
(`fittedX/fittedY`), the fraction, and the residual are byte-identical
before and after.

**Why it is owed.** The 2026-08-28 admit-with-fraction decision
(`q-calibration-design.md:440-457`) bound the app to carry the exclusion
evidence *on the product*, so it "survives export and reopen" and "reach[es]
the reader who sees the number." It shipped as a scalar. A scalar cannot
distinguish a scattered exclusion from a spatially clustered one — which is
precisely the gap open item "the trimmed fit is blind to spatially clustered
failure" names (`open-items.md`, origin-fit holes (c)). The per-position mask
is the same disclosure decision carried from a number to a map.

**What it touches, and who owns the state.**
- `OriginMaps` (`Core/Data/Calibration.swift:433`) — a new optional field
  (D1 below) and its init parameter. `OriginMaps` is the owner of the state; it
  already owns `excludedFraction`, so the mask lives beside it, not in `AppState`.
- The two `OriginMaps` constructors in `OriginCalibration.swift` (`:540`,
  `:594`) — pass `fitted.kept` through.
- The product surface that displays the origin fit (the origin-map display /
  workspace inspector). Presentation, D4 below.
- **Not touched this increment:** `BraggVectorEMDWriter.swift` / `H5Reader.swift`
  (sidecar/EMD I/O). Persistence is deferred — D3.

**Off by default.** Trivially satisfied: this increment is disclosure only.
No analysis reads the mask to decide anything, so no computed number can move.
The moment a later increment makes the mask *gate* a computation (e.g. refusing
to consume an interpolated origin), that increment is a Gate D of its own with
an independent refuter.

**The tests, written and broken first.**
- `originValidity` carries `TrimmedFit.kept` unchanged from a trimmed fit —
  break-first by dropping the field at the constructor → red.
- The new field and the existing scalar cannot silently disagree: assert
  `excludedFraction == 1 - mask.trueCount / mask.count` on a fit with a planted
  clustered outlier set, and that the mask marks exactly the planted positions.
- Nil-when-not-from-a-trim: an imported py4DSTEM / file / session `OriginMaps`
  carries `originValidity == nil`, mirroring the `excludedFraction` nil contract
  (`Calibration.swift:446-449`) — the app must not invent a mask nobody computed.
- Reconcile the case count against `func test` per file (the stale-bundle trap,
  `open-items.md`); grep the run log for each new test's own name.

**Gate.** Disclosure only → **unit gate** (`tools/run-tests.sh unit`, plus
`core` for the package build). Not a Gate D trigger: no scientific number moves,
placement/presentation only. Stated as unverified on screen until the owner
drives the display (D4).

**Landed 2026-09-17 (Core disclosure), unattended.** `OriginMaps.originValidity:
[Bool]?` added beside `excludedFraction`; populated from `TrimmedFit.kept` at the
two `OriginCalibration` trimmed-fit constructors (`tiledRun`, `run`). Tests:
`CalibrationDisclosureTests` pins the init carry and the nil contract (default +
`appOriginMaps` bridge); `ProbeSizeTests` drives `tiledRun`/`run` on a
displaced-beam fixture and pins the **production** carry — the mask marks the
three trimmed positions and equals an independent recompute of the trim on the
pipeline's own measured origins. Break-first: M1 (init drops the field) and M2
(bridge invents a mask) redden the disclosure tests; **M3 (both production carries
reverted to nil — the feature dead) reddens both production-path tests** and no
other. Gates: unit **693 / 0 / 2 = 695**, core exit 0, inventory exit 0.

**The adversarial pass earned its keep.** An independent 4-lens review
(no-number-moves, faithful-carry, wire-format-frozen, tests-honest) confirmed the
first three clean and **caught a real hole in the fourth**: the original three
tests built `OriginMaps` by hand, so they exercised only the init assignment —
reverting the two production carries left them all green (M3 would have passed).
The `ProbeSizeTests` production-path tests above are the fix, and a tautological
mask⇄fraction test was removed. **Still owed: D4 (on-screen) and the step-3
measurement.**

**Step-3 verdict — does it earn its place (measured when it lands).** The claim:
on a fit whose exclusions cluster spatially, the mask reveals the cluster; on a
fit with scattered exclusions at the *same* `excludedFraction`, the scalar is
identical and only the mask differs. The design cites `Particle_1…bin8` (2.19 px
kept vs 18.47 px full-scan, `q-calibration-design.md:463`) as a real clustered
case. **Measurement dependency:** that cube is gitignored multi-GB data not
resident here (`open-items.md`, disk notes); the verdict is measured on the
owner's data or a synthetic clustered-vs-scattered pair built in the test, and
this file records which was used when the increment lands.

## Decisions owed to the owner

Defaults are proposed; each is marked so the owner can overrule on sight.

- **D1 — representation.** `originValidity: [Bool]?` aligned index-for-index
  with `fittedX/fittedY` (`true` = a kept measurement). *Proposed default;
  mirrors the internal `kept` exactly.*
- **D2 — semantics.** The mask means the robust trim's `kept`: `false` = this
  position's fitted origin is interpolated from neighbours, not from a kept
  measurement — it does **not** separately distinguish "unmeasurable" from
  "measured-but-trimmed". *Proposed default; the honest minimal meaning.* Owner:
  whether a later increment should split those.
- **D3 — sidecar persistence (a wire-format decision, plan §8, owner's).** This
  increment carries the mask **in memory + on the product only**; it writes no
  sidecar/EMD field, so a restored session's mask reads nil — consistent with
  "the fit-anyway mark does not survive a round trip" (`open-items.md`). Adding
  the field touches the parked `BraggVectorEMDWriter` split (high HDF5 risk).
  Owner: whether/when to persist it.
- **D4 — on-screen.** A minimal validity indicator on the origin-map display (a
  count, or an excluded-position overlay). *Presentation — owner drives it;*
  unverified on screen until then.
