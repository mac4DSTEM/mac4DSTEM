## Surprising

1. grains_12_one_annulus (12 spots on one 41.2 px circle): HEAD accepts a dead-ring profile fit (I1 1.13, ring sigma 0.25, central Gaussian 137 px)
   centred right but 63.6 % small in R; the existing test passes it because it only tests a/b. Under (a) and ab the fit is a different dead
   ring (I1 = 0 exactly, sigmas 3.77 / 4.51 px, a = b = 50.20, +21.8 % in R) that is accepted too, and (b)'s floor guard no longer sees it.
   So (a) turns a floor-sigma dead ring into an I1 = 0 dead ring that the registered b-check cannot catch; on the tool, only b alone refuses it.
2. HEAD's profile fit is not at its optimum on some clean fixtures: asymmetric_background ends 0.247 % off the plant and the (a) start reaches
   it to 1e-8 residual. On the sweep, 19 of 24 unbroken rings and 4 of 8 gapped R = 80 rings move < 1e-3 % in (a+b)/2, but 5 unbroken
   rings move 0.003-0.039 % and the 4 gapped R = 80 rings 0.076-0.120 % (all to a lower residual). P3's 0.01 % no-move clause was written as
   if HEAD's fit were the optimum.
3. The tool's 48-ring sweep never called fitBestAvailable. Called with the same pattern/centre/annulus, HEAD keeps the conic on 16 of 48
   (all 16 gapped rings with R >= 120), so the sweep now shows HEAD's own profile stage failing its residual cap on clean data; (a) accepts
   all 16 (profile on 48 of 48).
4. The 8 sweep circles and several spot circles show large theta moves; theta is undefined there (a/b = 1 to 1e-9 or better) so those are
   not evidence of anything, but they are why a flat "<= 1e-3 rad in theta" cannot be applied to circles.
