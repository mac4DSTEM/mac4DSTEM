# 033 — v3.1 leads with the origin validity mask, scoped disclosure-only

Dates: 2026-09-17 (lead chosen; pre-registration written)

Status: live

## Decision

v3.1 (Calibration foundation, `ROADMAP.md` priority-1) is a four-item theme,
sequenced least-risk first because Gate B capacity is one campaign at a time.
The owner chose (2026-09-17) the **origin validity mask** to lead. It is scoped
**disclosure-only**: surface the per-position `kept` mask the robust origin fit
already computes (`TrimmedFit.kept`, `OriginCalibration.swift:188`) onto the
product (`OriginMaps`), where today only the derived scalar `excludedFraction`
survives. The fitted origins, the fraction and the residual are byte-identical
before and after, so **no shipped scientific number moves** — the increment is a
**unit gate**, not a Gate D. Sidecar/EMD persistence of the mask is **deferred**
to the owner's wire-format decision (plan §8) and is not part of this increment.
The remaining three items (CoM centre in `probeSize`; `get_origin_friedel`;
vacuum probe from a separate scan) each carry their own gate and are
pre-registered before their code.

## Why

The mask completes a decision already made, not a new one: the 2026-08-28
admit-with-fraction ruling (ADR-adjacent, `q-calibration-design.md:440-457`)
bound the app to carry the exclusion evidence *on the product* so it survives
export and reaches the reader who sees the number. It shipped as a scalar, which
cannot separate a scattered exclusion from a spatially clustered one — the exact
blind spot open item origin-fit hole (c) names. Leading with it lands the v3.1
pre-registration scaffolding at the lowest risk: the mask is one already-computed
field away, so the single Gate B slot is saved for the larger detection-side
items (the vacuum probe, the Friedel origin) rather than spent here.

## Governs

`docs/v3-features.md#calibration-v31` (item 1 in full, items 2–4
sequenced); the origin-validity-mask increment's gate (unit, not Gate D) and
its scope boundary (no `BraggVectorEMDWriter`/`H5Reader` change this increment).
